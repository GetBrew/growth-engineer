HeyReach runs LinkedIn outreach: connection requests, messages and InMail,
sent from one or many LinkedIn sender accounts that a campaign rotates
across, so volume scales with seats instead of pushing one profile past
LinkedIn's limits. Replies from every sender land in one unified inbox, and
campaigns, lists, senders and conversations are all reachable over the
public API. Only conversations that belong to HeyReach campaigns sync into
the inbox endpoints.
