Profound is an AI marketing platform. [AI Marketer, also called Aim](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/series-d), combines company context and AI Search data to identify opportunities and coordinate Agents. [Context Manager](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/introducing-context-manager-in-profound) supplies brand and personal context, while marketers direct and review the work.

[Answer Engine Insights](https://www.tryprofound.com/features/answer-engine-insights) tracks brand visibility and citations in AI answers. [Prompt Volumes](https://www.tryprofound.com/features/prompt-volumes) helps teams research demand, [Agent Analytics](https://www.tryprofound.com/features/agent-analytics) tracks AI crawler and referral activity, and [Profound Agents](https://www.tryprofound.com/features/agents) runs reusable marketing workflows. The hosted MCP exposes discovery, reporting and Agent operations to external AI clients.

On September 15, 2026, Profound announced a [$180M Series D at a $1.8B valuation](https://www.tryprofound.com/newsroom/profound-raises-usd180m-series-d-at-usd1-8b-valuation-to-build-the-ai-platform-for-marketing-teams), co-led by Sequoia Capital and Kleiner Perkins.
