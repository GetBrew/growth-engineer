Bridge is an AI meeting platform. It schedules meetings through booking
pages, hosts them on its own video calls or joins Zoom, Google Meet and
Teams, and keeps notes, transcripts and action items for every meeting.
Bridge also remembers the people and companies a team meets, so earlier
conversations can be searched and briefed before the next one.

The hosted MCP server covers the whole meeting lifecycle: free time and
booking pages, pre-meeting briefs and the meeting Notepad, Bridge video
meetings and invitations, notes and transcripts, follow-up plans and Bridge
agents, and search across people, companies and topics. It signs in with
OAuth and is not available on the Starter plan or a trial.
